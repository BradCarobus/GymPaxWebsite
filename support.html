Frequently Asked Questions

Open the app, tap the "+" button to start a new workout session. Search for or browse exercises, then tap an exercise to add it. Enter your sets, reps, and weight for each set. Tap "Finish" when you're done to save the session to your history.

A Pax is a custom workout pack — a pre-built collection of exercises you or your crew can use as a routine. You can create your own Pax, share it with others, and load any Pax to kick off a workout session instantly.

Navigate to the Pax tab and tap "Create Pax." Give it a name, then add exercises in the order you want to perform them. Once saved, use the share button to send it to friends via any messaging app or AirDrop.

We believe you shouldn't need to hand over personal information just to track your workouts. Gym Pax stores everything locally on your device, so there's no account to sign up for, no email to verify, and no data leaving your phone.

Your workout data is included in your device's standard iCloud or iTunes backup automatically. To transfer to a new phone, restore from your backup and Gym Pax will have all your history. You can also export your data from the app's Settings screen.

To delete individual workouts, swipe left on a session in your History tab and tap "Delete." To erase all data, go to Settings > Delete All Data. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
